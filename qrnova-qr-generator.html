Free QR Code Generator

QRNova is a free QR code generator that lets you create QR codes for websites, text, contact details, Wi-Fi networks, payments, and more. As an online QR code generator, it runs entirely in your browser, so you can generate a QR code, customize its appearance, and download it in a few seconds — no software to install and no account to create.

Whether you need a simple URL QR code for a business card, a Wi-Fi QR code for guests, or a custom QR code generator for a marketing campaign, QRNova gives you a straightforward way to create QR code online without unnecessary steps.

How to Create a QR Code

  1. Select a QR type that matches what you want to share.
  2. Enter the required data for that type, such as a link or Wi-Fi password.
  3. Customize colors, size, margin, and error correction if needed.
  4. Select Generate QR Code to build your QR code.
  5. Download your QR code as PNG, JPG, WebP, or SVG.
  6. Test-scan your QR code with a phone before printing or sharing it.
